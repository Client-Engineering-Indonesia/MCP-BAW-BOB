# Building

**Source:** https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/dbaoc?topic=automation-building

## Overview

Iteratively build and test your business processes and their supporting artifacts.

## Key Topics

### Creating a Workflow Automation
Design and develop workflow automations and workflow automation services to optimize core business processes.

### Processes
A process is a set of related activities, along with supporting information such as data and content. The activities can be part of a structured flow, or ad-hoc activities that are not part of a structured flow.

### Services
Services provide functions for a business process. You can discover external services and use them in your process.

### Business Objects and Variables
Variables capture the business data that is used by activities in a process, service flow or human service.

### Running and Debugging
Authoring processes is an iterative process that can involve several playback sessions. You can run, test, and debug your processes and services in the designer's Inspector.

### Deploying and Installing Workflow Automations
You can run a workflow automation directly or call it from other business automations and external clients. You can also publish a workflow as an automation service that you can call and reuse in a consistent way, for example from a user interface. The workflow automations run on workflow servers.
