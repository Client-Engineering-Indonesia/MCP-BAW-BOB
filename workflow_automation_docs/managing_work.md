# Managing Work

**Source:** https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/dbaoc?topic=automation-managing-work

## Overview

Manage and work on your tasks, participate in workflows and cases to complete work and, with associated permission, use dashboards to manage and evaluate the workload and performance of your teams.

## Work Portals

Two work portals are available for managing and completing work, supported on both traditional and container Business Automation Workflow environments:

### Workplace
- Offers a newer, modern, and easily customizable experience
- Enables creation of custom-built work environments tuned to specific needs
- Flexible design supports intuitive management of tasks, workflows, and cases in one place
- Improves productivity through better work organization

### Process Portal
- Earlier, coach-based work portal for tasks and process instances only (no cases)
- May be more familiar for existing configurations and legacy implementations
- Operational and fully supported but might be targeted for deprecation as Workplace becomes primary

## Workplace Advantages

### Common Work Portal
Supported in both traditional and container Business Automation Workflow environments, providing consistent portal across platforms and simplifying transitions between deployment models.

### Single Place for Different Work Types
Consolidates process and case work into one interface, enabling orchestration, prioritization, tracking, and completion of all work types in a unified view. Tasks, workflows, and cases share consistent user experience. Managers can oversee team workloads and performance through built-in dashboards.

### Advanced Searches
Supports advanced searches for process and case instances in both federated and nonfederated environments through Elasticsearch or OpenSearch indexing.

### Customizable and Reusable Views
Provides flexible customization options for task lists, including sorting, filtering, and layout adjustments. Custom views can be saved for reuse or shared with teams, supporting tailored work experiences and consistent team configurations.

## Key Topics

- **Managing work in Workplace** - Consolidated user experience for orchestrating, prioritizing, tracking, and completing different work types
- **Managing work in Process Portal** - Earlier coach-based interface for participating in processes, working on tasks, and using dashboards
